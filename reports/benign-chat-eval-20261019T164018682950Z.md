## Deduplication and selection

Method: lowercase + whitespace collapse + edge punctuation strip; exact set match and same-first-50-char bucket with SequenceMatcher ratio >= 0.95; deduplicated against train.parquet and validation.parquet only (test split never read) plus within-dataset and cross-dataset checks
- **dolly**: duplicate_within: 262, exact_match_reference: 3
- **oasst1**: duplicate_cross_dataset: 3

Selection: sort eligible deduplicated candidates by sha256(normalized text) ascending, take first 100 per dataset; selected 100 Dolly + 100 OASST1 rows (n=200).

## Limitations

Public dataset prompts are not production traffic: English-only, single-turn, and drawn from two sources; n=200 supports only an overall block-rate estimate (roughly +/-4-7 percentage points), not subgroup, recall, calibration, or non-English claims. Eligibility requires reviewer-confirmed benign quality, but crowd annotations are imperfect. Intervals overlapping the validation benchmark do not prove the rates are equal. This slice is single-use: if mitigation is ever pursued it becomes development data and cannot support post-fix claims.
